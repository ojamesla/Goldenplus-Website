GOLDENPLUS ELECTRONICS · SINCE 2008

Building the future of embedded HMI.

Founded in 2008, Goldenplus Electronics combines engineering, product design and manufacturing experience with visual GUI development.

OUR STORY

From system integration to complete HMI solutions.

Goldenplus Electronics began with LED lighting and control systems before expanding into 32-bit ARM-based embedded systems and TFT display applications.

Today we focus on connecting display hardware, development software and customer applications through flexible, integrated solutions.

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones that shaped Goldenplus.

2008

Company founded

Goldenplus Electronics was established with a focus on electronic product design and manufacturing, building a foundation in engineering and system integration.

2008–2014

LED lighting & control systems

Goldenplus specialized in LED lighting systems and architectural and landscape lighting integration, developing its Taiwan-based engineering and manufacturing capabilities.

2014

Embedded displays & GUI development

With the rise of 32-bit ARM processors, the company shifted toward embedded TFT display applications and began developing its own graphical interface development environment.

2025

Cross-platform visual GUI platform

Goldenplus introduced a cross-CPU/MCU visual GUI development direction, applying low-code and no-code ideas to streamline embedded interface design and programming.

Today

Embedded HMI ecosystem

Goldenplus brings together display modules, Brilliant Display Editor (BDE), and application integration to help engineers create embedded user interfaces.
