Goldenplus at Taiwan Smart Agriweek 2026

A look back at our embedded HMI technology and demonstration solutions exhibited in Taipei.

← Back to News & Updates

EventTaiwan Smart Agriweek 2026
DatesSeptember 8–10, 2026
LocationTaipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 1, Taiwan

Embedded HMI solutions in focus

Goldenplus Electronics showcased embedded human-machine interface (HMI) technology at Taiwan Smart Agriweek 2026. Our exhibition displays included the Omni HMI and Omni PLC families, Aeternus HMI products, and Brilliant Display Editor (BDE), our visual GUI development environment.

The booth highlighted how graphical interfaces and embedded control hardware can be combined for equipment monitoring and automation applications. Visitors could explore a range of display sizes, control interfaces, and demonstrations on site.

Exhibition highlights

  • Omni Display: HMI and PLC-oriented interface systems presented with integrated control features.
  • Aeternus Display: embedded displays designed for user-interface and peripheral integration applications.
  • Brilliant Display Editor: graphical interface design and software development workflow.
  • Application demonstrations: interactive control panels and prototype equipment displayed at the booth.
